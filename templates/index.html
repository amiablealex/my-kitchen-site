What it does

My Kitchen is your simple stock manager, personal recipe database, and creative assistant. It asks "What's in stock?" and shows you which of your personal recipe database you can cook right now. Nothing take your fancy? An integrated AI model takes your brief and generates a high-quality creative recipe - verified against your actual live stock list.

  • {{ icon('shelves') }}

    Cook from what's actually in

    Keep stock with a single tap as you shop and cook. Configure a bank of staples like oil and spices, and meals get built around what you have.

  • {{ icon('search') }}

    See what you can make right now

    At “Lets cook”, My Kitchen surfaces your saved recipes you can cook immediately from your current stock. A great meal you've made before doesn't get forgotten, and you skip a needless trip to the generation model.

  • {{ icon('cooking') }}

    Fresh ideas when you want them

    Nothing takes your fancy? Generate. It builds a brief from your kitchen, your constraints and a creative angle, then returns two distinctly different recipes for you to choose from.

  • {{ icon('kitchen') }}

    Every recipe linked to your kitchen

    Each loose-text ingredient line is matched to your real catalogue, which is the engine that lets the app reason about what's cookable. Write your own recipes in too, and re-configure the ingredient links by hand if the resolver ever gets it wrong.

  • {{ icon('group') }}

    Built for the whole household

    Add everyone to the app, and you can all see each other's favourite recipes. Configure your own dietary needs, allergies and preferences, choose who you're cooking for and any generated recipes adjust accordingly. Always check allergies yourself, because an AI is never a safety net.

  • {{ icon('lock') }}

    Yours, on your own hardware

    Self-hosted and private: your stock, recipes and history never leave your network. Only a short ingredient brief goes to the model when you generate, and a local-model option even keeps that local. More on data ›

Run it on your own hardware
