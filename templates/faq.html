FAQ

Common questions

The short answers. For the longer ones, see the docs.

Do I need Home Assistant?

No, it also runs as a plain Docker container behind any reverse proxy. Home Assistant is the easiest path; it's a one-click install, easy updates and secure remote access.

Is it free?

The app is open source and free to run. The only running cost is your LLM provider's API usage, which is small with the lightweight default model, or zero if you run a local model.

Does my data leave my network?

Your stock, recipes, history and accounts stay on your hardware. The only thing sent out is a short generation brief (ingredients and constraints) to your chosen LLM, and only when you generate. A local-model option avoids even that. See Privacy & data.

Which LLM does it use?

A lightweight hosted model by default, through a swappable provider adapter. You supply your own API key, and you can point it at a different provider, or a local model.

Can more than one person use it?

Yes. Add everyone in the house, each with their own sign-in details, dietary preferences, and their own favourites. Pick who you're cooking for and the recipe brief adjusts.

Can I add my own recipes?

Yes. Write them straight into the app, with ingredients picked from your catalogue so they join your cookable suggestions like the other recipes.

Is it safe for allergies?

Allergies are emphasised as absolute exclusions in the brief, but an AI is not a safety guarantee. Recipes generated with an allergy in play carry a visible reminder to check the ingredients yourself, and you always should.
